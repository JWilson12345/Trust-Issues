Every 50 levels, in two parts

Nine bosses. Zero mercy.

Every boss is a different idea: geysers, wall-climbing, weak points, arcade volleyball, crushers, riding a dragon, walking the ceiling, a memory duel and a final exam. Beat a Part I boss and you keep its power. Part II bosses guard the Tower of Lies.

Final boss · Level 500

The Overlord is watching.

He has narrated every death since level one. At the top of the Tower of Lies you finally meet him: lasers, fire, lightning and every trick he ever built, all at once. Double jump, dash, Spite and Salt Strike all matter. What is behind the mask? You will have to climb to find out.
